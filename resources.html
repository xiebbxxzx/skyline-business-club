How Do I Qualify for ICDC or NLC?

National conference qualification is based on your performance at State.

Place 1st, 2nd, or 3rd in any event at State to qualify for your organization's national conference: ICDC for DECA or NLC for FBLA. This includes testing events.

Region and invitational placements do not count toward ICDC or NLC qualification.
