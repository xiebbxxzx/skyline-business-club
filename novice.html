What is DECA/FBLA?

DECA

DECA is a business competition organization focused on areas such as marketing, finance, hospitality, management, and entrepreneurship. Members compete through roleplays and case studies, prepared projects and presentations, and online simulations; many events also include a career cluster exam.

Official DECA Website

FBLA

FBLA is a business leadership organization focused on career skills, technology, communication, entrepreneurship, and academic business events. Members compete through objective tests, presentations, projects, and leadership events.

Official FBLA Website
