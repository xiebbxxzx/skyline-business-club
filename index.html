What is this club?

Skyline Business Club

Skyline Business Club is the home for Skyline students interested in DECA, FBLA, business competitions, leadership, career skills, and community. As the largest and most successful DECA/FBLA chapter in Utah, our members compete across regional, state, and national circuits while building real-world business experience together.

Regional, state, and national recognition across DECA and FBLA competition circuits.

What is DECA/FBLA?

DECA

DECA is a business competition organization focused on areas such as marketing, finance, hospitality, management, and entrepreneurship. Members compete through roleplays, written projects, presentations, and exams.

Official DECA Website

FBLA

FBLA is a business leadership organization focused on career skills, technology, communication, entrepreneurship, and academic business events. Members compete through objective tests, presentations, projects, and leadership events.

Official FBLA Website
